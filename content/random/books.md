## Book Recommendations

I've enjoyed reading since childhood but most of what I read was for the school curriculum, or non-fiction.

When I started university in 2014, I mostly stopped reading novels as I wanted to focus on my studies and so only read academic works.

Over the past few years, I've gotten back into reading and I'm thoroughly enjoying it...so I decided to put together a list of my absolute favourites, along with reviews.

I find large books quite daunting - I'm more of a short-story enthusiast.
But you will find a large book or two in the list!
